# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

bonsai-ninja welcomes constructive participation from people of every
background and experience level. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss
technical evidence directly, and make room for questions and disagreement.

Helpful participation includes:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- separating criticism of code or ideas from criticism of people;
- respecting privacy, disclosure windows, and source-code ownership;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting the record;
- helping keep issues, reviews, and discussions focused.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate intimidation, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.
Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, proprietary
source, or uncoordinated vulnerability details.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release coordination, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone
is representing the project in a public venue.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@gromhacks.com` with the subject
`bonsai-ninja conduct report`. Include links, dates, context, and the outcome
you are requesting. Do not use a public issue for a report that would expose a
target or private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the project and its community. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
